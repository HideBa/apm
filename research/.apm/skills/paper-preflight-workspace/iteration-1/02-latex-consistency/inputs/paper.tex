\section{Introduction}
GANs have become central to generative modeling, and their behaviour under
distribution shift is widely studied. We optimise a Support Vector Machine (SVM)
baseline before turning to deep models. Recurrent architectures such as the RNN
process sequences, and we perform fine-tuning of large models on downstream tasks
\cite{krizhevsky2017}.

\section{Background}
A Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) pits a generator against a discriminator.
We later compare against Support Vector Machines (SVM) trained on the same data.
Model behavior after finetuning is our main focus, and we optimize the objective
with Adam \cite{he2016}.
